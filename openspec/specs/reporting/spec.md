## Requirements
### Requirement: Net Revenue Calculation
The system SHALL reflect the true net revenue by deducting the value of returned items from gross revenue reports.

#### Scenario: Revenue deduction
- **WHEN** the Manager Dashboard shows total revenue for a period
- **THEN** the value returned during that period is dynamically subtracted to show the Net Revenue
